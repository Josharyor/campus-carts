\# Campus Carts



> A simple solution for campus vendors to streamline stock tracking, cart totals, and receipt generation.



\## Product Vision



Campus Carts is a Python-based application designed to help student businesses and pop-up vendors manage their sales more efficiently.



The application will provide simple tools for tracking stock, adding products to a customer's cart, calculating totals, and generating receipts.



\## Problem Statement



Student businesses and small pop-up vendors often manage sales manually.



This can lead to:



\- Incorrect stock counts

\- Calculation mistakes

\- Slow checkout processes

\- Difficulty tracking products

\- Manually written receipts



Campus Carts aims to provide a simple and accessible solution to these problems.



\## Target Audience



## User Personas

### Persona 1 — Student Food Vendor

*Name:* Daniel

*Role:* University student selling food on campus

*Needs:*

- Track food stock
- Quickly calculate customer orders
- Generate receipts
- Reduce calculation errors

*Problem:*

Daniel currently uses a notebook to track his stock and manually calculates customer totals.

*How Campus Cart Helps:*

Campus Cart provides stock tracking and automatic cart calculations.

### Persona 2 — Pop-Up Clothing Vendor

*Name:* Sarah

*Role:* Student selling clothing at university events

*Needs:*

- Track available products
- Monitor stock levels
- Process sales quickly
- Know the total value of each sale

*Problem:*

Sarah has difficulty keeping track of different products and quantities during busy events.

*How Campus Cart Helps:*

Campus Cart provides a simple way to manage products and process sales.

\## Key Value Propositions

Campus Cart provides affordable and simple tools that help small campus vendors manage sales efficiently.
\### Simple Stock Tracking



Vendors can keep track of available products and quantities.



\### Automatic Cart Totals



The application can calculate the total cost of products added to a customer's cart.



\### Receipt Generation



Campus Carts will generate a simple receipt showing purchased products, quantities and the total amount.



\### Easy to Use



The application will use a simple command-line interface so that users can navigate it without complicated software.



\## Proposed Features



\- Add products

\- View products

\- Update stock

\- Add products to cart

\- Remove products from cart

\- Calculate cart total

\- Generate receipts

\- View basic sales information
